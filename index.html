          How to play
          
      
          
        
      
          
    
          
    
          Guess the school of thought, from psychology or philosophy, in 6 tries.
          
    
            
      
          • You start with one clue. Each wrong guess unlocks another, up to 5. The last clue names the key thinkers.
            • 
      
          • Start typing and pick your guess from the list.
            • 
    
          

    
          Reading the colours
          
    
          
      ✓ Match
      ≈ Close
      ✗ Not close
    
          
    
            
      
          • Specialty: the area it works in, like Ethics, Mind or Clinical. A match if the specialties are exactly the same, close if at least one is shared (shared ones are underlined).
            • 
      
          • Began: a match within 20 years, close within 100. The arrow shows whether the answer began later (↑) or earlier (↓).
            • 
      
          • Origin: a match for the same country, close for the same continent.
            • 
      
          • Type: School, Movement, Tradition, Theory, Therapy or Method. A match if it's the same type.
            • 
    
          

    
          Daily and Practice
          
    
            
      
          • Daily: two puzzles each day, one Philosophy and one Psychology. They're the same for everyone and new at midnight, and each has its own stats and streak.
            • 
      
          • Practice: as many rounds as you like, in Philosophy or Psychology.
            • 
      
          • Every answer you meet is saved in your Encyclopedia for revision.
